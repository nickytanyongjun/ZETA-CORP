What we do

We build simple software for real businesses.

Good software. Made affordable.

Zeta builds software for businesses like restaurants and shops. We make tools that are easy to use, so your staff don't need training or a manual to figure it out.

We build for the people who use the software every day — the cashier during the lunch rush, the kitchen reading an order, the manager closing up at night. Before we ship anything, we ask one simple question: does this make their day easier?

We're a small studio, and we'd rather build a few things really well than many things half-finished. Right now that's one product already helping businesses every day, with a second one on the way.
